COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions.

Everything you need to know before placing an order.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope and complexity. Browse fixed starting prices on our Order & Pay page, or contact us for a custom quote.

How long does a typical project take?

A standard website is delivered in 7 to 14 working days once payment, content and feedback are provided on time. Larger custom software and CRM projects take longer and are scoped individually.

How does payment work?

Payment is split into two equal parts: a 50% deposit when you place the order, and the remaining 50% when the completion notice is given. Payments are made in KES via Paystack (M-Pesa, Visa or Mastercard).

Who owns the final product?

Ownership of the completed deliverable transfers to you after full payment. Source code, admin access and credentials are handed over at that point.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Defects reported within 30 days of deployment are fixed under warranty. Ongoing maintenance after that is optional and billed separately.

What kind of work does RiM Services do?

Websites and web systems, custom software, CRM platforms, AI assistants, GIS / geospatial mapping, and cloud & IT infrastructure support. See our Services section for details.
